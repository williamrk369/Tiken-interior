Tiken Interior

L-Shaped Living & Dining + Front Room

Designed for the 23′ × 23′ house on site: a 23′ × 14′ hall plus the 11′ × 9′ L-extension form one living and dining space, with an 11′ × 9′ front room at the entrance. The open hip roof gets a suspended ceiling at 9′-4″, with a raised tray over the living area, a dome over the dining table, and an LED edge light running around every wall.

Ceiling specification
  • Suspended ceiling: 12.5 mm gypsum board (moisture-resistant) on a galvanised steel grid, hung from the timber rafters and wall plate. Underside at 2850 mm (9′-4″) everywhere, with 50 mm glass-wool insulation laid on top to cut heat from the metal roof.
  • Living tray: rectangular opening with a 600 mm (2′) band, rising 400 mm into the roof void to 3250 mm. Dining dome: Ø2.0 m circular opening rising to the same height, with a Ø2.4 m drum.
  • Cove light: 24 V COB LED tape, 14 W/m, 3000 K tunable white (2200–6500 K), in an aluminium channel 60 mm behind a 70 mm upstand – no visible dots.
  • Edge light: 60 mm shadow-gap profile with opal diffuser around every wall of the L-shaped space and the front room (≈ 38 m in total). It grazes the walls and the fluted walnut TV wall.
  • Downlights: 15 × trimless 7 W, 2700 K, CRI 95: 22° beams grazing the TV wall, one aimed at the dining artwork, 36° elsewhere.
  • Ventilation: the existing breeze-block vents stay visible below the ceiling line so the rooms still breathe. The roof void above the ceiling should get its own eave vents.
  • Control: 5 dimmable circuits (DALI-2 / Casambi) with Day · Evening · Cinema scenes.
