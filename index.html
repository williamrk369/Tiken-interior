Living Room — Floating Cove Ceiling

A warm-minimal living room built around the ceiling: a dropped gypsum band frames a raised centre field, washed by a hidden LED cove, while a 60 mm shadow-gap light traces every edge of the room.

Ceiling specification
  • Floating band: 2 × 12.5 mm gypsum on metal furring, 700 mm wide, underside at 2700 mm; centre field left at slab height (≈3000 mm) for a 300 mm step.
  • Cove light: 24 V COB LED tape, 14 W/m, 3000 K tunable white (2200–6500 K), in an aluminium channel 60 mm behind a 70 mm upstand – no visible dots.
  • Edge light: 60 mm shadow-gap profile with opal diffuser on all four walls; grazes the walls and the fluted walnut panel. Doubles as the hidden curtain track.
  • Downlights: 8 × trimless 7 W, 36° (22° grazing the TV wall), 2700 K, CRI 95, UGR < 16.
  • Control: 5 dimmable circuits (DALI-2 / Casambi) with Day · Evening · Cinema scenes.
