    The editing rules
    1. Keep A-roll in one setup. Punch in ~1.2× on emphasis words instead of cutting.
    2. Captions word by word, one font family. Style only the key word (serif italic, brand colour or bigger).
    3. Change the visual every 1.5–3 s: B-roll, a card or an infographic.
    4. No hard cuts: zoom-through, blur, slide and morph, each with a whoosh.
    5. Nothing sits still: a slow 2–5 % scale or drift on everything, over a living background.
    6. End on a keyword-comment CTA and an animated handle card. Keep a small handle in the corner throughout.
